# Indicative Imaging and Treatment Costs in Nepal

These figures are **indicative only** and vary substantially between government
and private facilities and between regions. They are provided so a patient can
plan, not as a quotation.

## Imaging

| Investigation | Indicative range (NPR) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Chest X-ray | 300 - 800 | Widely available; high value in the TB differential |
| CT head, plain | 3,000 - 6,000 | Widely available; first-line in trauma and acute stroke |
| CT head with contrast | 5,000 - 10,000 | |
| **MRI brain, plain** | **8,000 - 15,000** | Concentrated in urban centres |
| MRI brain with contrast | 12,000 - 22,000 | |
| MRI brain with MRA/MRV | 15,000 - 25,000 | For vascular assessment |
| EEG | 1,500 - 4,000 | |

The MRI figure is consistent with the range cited in the project's own
background research (NPR 8,000-15,000).

## Putting that in context

Nepal's per-capita income means an MRI brain scan can **exceed a month's income
for many households**. Added to this are:

- Transport for the patient and at least one attendant, frequently 8-12 hours
  each way from remote districts
- Accommodation and food in the city, often for several days
- Lost earnings for the patient and the accompanying family member
- Follow-up visits

**The true cost of a scan is therefore considerably more than the scan fee**,
and this is the reason many patients do not complete referrals. Any advice that
ignores this is advice that will not be followed.

## Consultation and treatment (indicative)

| Item | Indicative range (NPR) |
|---|---|
| Specialist outpatient consultation, government | 100 - 1,000 |
| Specialist outpatient consultation, private | 1,000 - 3,000 |
| Neurosurgery (varies enormously by procedure) | 150,000 - 800,000+ |
| Radiotherapy course | 100,000 - 400,000 |
| **TB treatment** | **Free via the National TB Programme** |
| Antiseizure medication, monthly | 300 - 3,000 |

## How to reduce cost for the patient

1. **Do the cheap tests first.** A chest X-ray at NPR 500 can substantially
   redirect the differential toward treatable TB and away from an expensive
   neurosurgical pathway.
2. **Use government facilities** where the wait is acceptable - TUTH,
   Bir/NAMS, BPKIHS and provincial hospitals charge far less.
3. **Enrol in health insurance** and follow the referral chain so claims are
   valid.
4. **Apply to the Bipanna Nagarik fund** through the hospital social service
   unit for qualifying conditions.
5. **Consider whether CT would answer the question.** For calcified
   neurocysticercosis, acute haemorrhage or trauma, CT is adequate, cheaper and
   closer.
6. **Avoid duplicate scans** by ensuring the patient carries their images.


> **Verify before relying on these figures.** Costs, entitlements and contact
> details in Nepal change frequently. The figures here are indicative, compiled
> on the review date above, and are intended to help a patient plan - not to
> quote as a guaranteed price. Confirm directly with the facility or scheme.
